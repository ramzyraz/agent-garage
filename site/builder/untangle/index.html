Private by design, and you can check

Untangle has no server and no upload. Your workbook is unzipped and read by JavaScript inside this browser tab, then forgotten when you close it. To check: load this page, turn off your Wi-Fi, then drop your file. It still works. The page counts anonymous visits (GoatCounter, no cookies), and never sends anything from your file.

Reads .xlsx and .xlsm (Excel 2007 and later; Google Sheets and LibreOffice can save as .xlsx). Old .xls files and password-protected workbooks need re-saving first. Untangle doesn't run macros. Source code.
